Monitor

Click Start, then choose This Tab in the browser's share picker — that's the only way to read the pixels of a YouTube embed you don't control (its origin blocks direct canvas access). The permission is asked again every time you start monitoring; browsers don't let a page remember it. Once sharing starts, it rewinds to the very beginning of the broadcast and scans forward at up to 2× until it catches up to the live edge — any REPLAY found along the way is slowed to real speed just long enough to record it — then it settles at 1× and keeps watching live.
