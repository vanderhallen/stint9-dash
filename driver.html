About this data

Source: the official Offizielles Ergebnis Rennen PDFs on nuerburgring-langstrecken-serie.de, one per 2026 round, parsed automatically. Refreshes weekly (and on demand via ↻ Update).
• Points in class use a fixed class-position scale (1st→25, 2nd→18, 3rd→15, 4th→12, 5th→10, 6th→8, 7th→6, 8th→4, 9th→2, 10th→1) — NLS does not print championship points on the result sheet, so this is a transparent, editable approximation.
• Best lap is the car's fastest lap (the result sheet lists one fastest lap per car, not per driver), attributed to each of the car's drivers.
• Age is not shown: no NLS entry list or result sheet publishes driver birthdates.
• The 24h Nürburgring race is included when its result PDF is reachable (best-effort — that site is bot-protected).
